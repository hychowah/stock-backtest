This pool pre-selects the 250 highest report EBITDA/EV yields. Use all stocks with cached prices for an unbiased ranking.

Steps

Steps run in order after the universe. A Filter keeps the stocks that pass. A Rank orders them and keeps its rank range.

    How this works

    The last Rank sets the final order and ranks; filters after it add no replacements. Each selected stock receives equal weight. Latest mode ranks at the latest cached close. Backtests rebalance on the selected fixed or random schedule.

    Auto-run works in Latest stock picks: turn it off to change several settings, then select Screen latest. A dated test runs from Add to queue.

    Sales-based operating yield spread: latest eligible annual sales × the simple average of operating margins from 3 consecutive annual reports ÷ screen-date EV, minus the US 10-year Treasury yield. Each annual operating margin is operating income ÷ sales. Use the latest saved Treasury yield on or before the screen date. Enter 5 for a spread of 5 percentage points. Missing years, operating inputs or Treasury yields fail this condition.

    Three-year metric divides current EV by mean EBITDA from three consecutive annual reports available at the screening date; stocks missing a year are excluded. Yield is EBITDA ÷ EV, the reciprocal of the multiple (EV ÷ EBITDA). A higher yield and a lower multiple both mean a cheaper stock. New strategies use all eligible stocks with cached prices. Recorded US legacy strategies retain their original candidate set.

    Offline latest screens use shared prepared data. Other latest chains and dated backtests use Original on the local service. Completed results keep the engine used and their original downloads.

    Dates and frequency

    Dates roll to the next cached session of the selected market. All returns use USD and compare with SPY.

    Advanced: repeat the same random dates

    Fresh random dates are picked on every run by default. Enable repeat mode to compare strategies on identical dates, or enter a previous result’s date-set number.

    How this works

    The final mark rolls back to a cached session. SPY is the S&P 500 total-return proxy. For HK/Japan, its comparison uses the latest completed US close available at the local close. Month-end dates keep the original day anchor. Equal weights reset at each rebalance. Custom schedules use the local service opened by the launcher.

    Random testing samples one trading date independently inside each rebalance block for every trial. Trial count defaults to 24 and is configurable (1–100). Each run draws fresh dates; repeating a date set is optional under Advanced. Actual holding intervals vary around the selected frequency; both strategy and paired SPY hold cash until the first sampled trade.

    Custom week/month interval: weeks support 1–104. Months support 1–24. Clear this option to use the frequency preset.
