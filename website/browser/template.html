Additional filters · match all conditions

Each filter searches one list: built-in filters, your saved variables, measures and financial parameters. Type a word such as P/E, revenue or margin, then choose a comparison and a value. Built-in filters work offline and update results automatically. A variable, measure or parameter moves the strategy to the local service, and auto-run pauses. Use two conditions on the same item for a range. Returns are trailing dividend-adjusted returns at the screen date. Enter 10 for 10%. Stocks with missing values fail that condition. Financial ratios need annual statement inputs. Choose Manage backtest data → Update data to get these inputs and prepare them automatically. P/E and P/B use screen-date market cap. PEG uses positive 3-year diluted EPS growth. ROE uses average annual equity.

Sales-based operating yield spread

Latest eligible annual sales × the simple average of operating margins from 3 consecutive annual reports ÷ screen-date EV, minus the US 10-year Treasury yield. Each annual operating margin is operating income ÷ sales. Use the latest saved Treasury yield on or before the screen date. Enter 5 for a spread of 5 percentage points. Missing years, operating inputs or Treasury yields fail this condition.

Advanced: repeat the same random dates

Fresh random dates are picked on every run by default. Enable repeat mode to compare strategies on identical dates, or enter a previous result’s date-set number.

Random testing samples one trading date independently inside each rebalance block for every trial. Trial count defaults to 24 and is configurable (1–100). Each run draws fresh dates; repeating a date set is optional under Advanced. Actual holding intervals vary around the selected frequency; both strategy and paired SPY hold cash until the first sampled trade.

Dates roll to the next cached SPY session; the final mark rolls back. Month-end dates keep the original day anchor. Equal weights reset at each rebalance. Custom schedules use the local service opened by the launcher.

Built-in latest screens use shared prepared data. Variable strategies and dated backtests use Original on the local service. Completed results keep the engine used and their original downloads.

Three-year metric divides current EV by mean EBITDA from three consecutive annual reports available at the screening date; stocks missing a year are excluded. Yield is the reciprocal of the selected multiple. The original candidate set remains restricted by the original single-year pre-screen; use all cached stocks for broader metric comparisons.
