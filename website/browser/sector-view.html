How sector weights are calculated

Each stock has equal weight at entry. Divide the sector's stock count by the actual book size. Average rebalances within each trial, then average complete trials equally. Empty books count as cash. Missing labels remain Unknown and stay in the denominator. Incomplete trials remain listed and are excluded from the combined average. These are entry allocations, not daily exposure or one combined portfolio. Labels are those recorded in the original result.
