How annual values are dated

Annual values use the latest eligible annual report: 90-day lag, availability strictly before the screen date, and at most 800 days old. Annual statistics require every consecutive fiscal year. Month lookbacks reconstruct an earlier date; they do not use quarterly or TTM accounts. Calendar-series statistics are not supported.
