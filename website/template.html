How to read these results

Each book starts on the first trading session in January and exits the following January. The 2026 book is marked through 20 October. Returns include dividends and assume no commissions. Stocks that stop trading stay in the results at their last price; identified bankruptcies would be total losses. The current pass includes 37 early trading stops and no identified bankruptcies.

The universe includes delisted US names on NYSE, Nasdaq and NYSE American. Minimum market cap: $100 million; minimum enterprise value: $50 million. Annual figures are used after a 90-day lag. Data and ratio quality affect these historical backtest results.
