Advanced: repeat the same random dates

Fresh random dates are picked on every run by default. Enable repeat mode to compare strategies on identical dates, or enter a previous result’s date-set number.

Random testing samples one trading date independently inside each rebalance block for every trial. Trial count defaults to 24 and is configurable (1–100). Each run draws fresh dates; repeating a date set is optional under Advanced. Actual holding intervals vary around the selected frequency; both strategy and paired SPY hold cash until the first sampled trade.

Dates roll to the next cached SPY session; the final mark rolls back. Month-end dates keep the original day anchor. Equal weights reset at each rebalance. Custom schedules use the local service opened by the launcher.

Year ranges are inclusive: 2015–2020 covers January 2015 to January 2021. Ranks 10–20 select up to 11 stocks per year.

Three-year metric divides current EV by mean EBITDA from three consecutive annual reports available at the screening date; stocks missing a year are excluded. How to read these results

Each book starts on the first trading session in January and exits the following January. The 2026 book is marked through 20 October. Returns include dividends and assume no commissions. Stocks that stop trading stay in the results at their last price; identified bankruptcies would be total losses. The original top-50 pass includes 37 early trading stops and no identified bankruptcies. Expanded picks may contain additional stops that have not had the same manual review.

The universe includes delisted US names on NYSE, Nasdaq and NYSE American. The saved baseline uses minimum market cap of $100 million and enterprise value of $50 million. Custom filters apply to prepared observations with cached prices. Annual figures are used after a 90-day lag. Data and ratio quality affect these historical backtest results.
